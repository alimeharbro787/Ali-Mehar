## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an administrative experience for premium maternity, infant nursing, and domestic care operations in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The aesthetic synthesizes clinical-grade rigor with bespoke hospitality and gentle nurturing. 

### Core Ethos
- **Maternal Dignity & Safety:** Interface spaces project calm reassurance, empathetic discretion, and clinical precision.
- **Riyadh Luxury & Heritage:** Deep, majestic greens harmonize with warm parchment tones and subtle champagne-gold filaments, evoking high-trust Arabian hospitality.
- **Operational Clarity:** High-density admin interfaces (nurse scheduling, vitals monitoring, billing, domestic service booking) maintain effortless scanning and low cognitive fatigue under demanding operational conditions.

### Design Movement: Warm Seraphic Minimalism
A hybrid blend of warm organic minimalism and high-end editorial clarity. Layouts prioritize generous whitespace, whisper-thin borders, tactile micro-elevations, and refined serif typography paired with hyper-legible geometric functional type.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative medical depth with warm maternal warmth and Gulf hospitality.

### Key Palettes & Color Roles
- **Primary (`#064E3B`, Deep Forest Green):** The commanding anchor color. Represents clinical security, health, and grounded tranquility. Used for navigation frames, primary buttons, major headings, and active state indicators.
- **Deep Sanctuary Shadow (`#022C22`):** Reserved for sidebar surfaces, dark status bars, and high-emphasis contextual menus.
- **Secondary (`#BE185D`, Warm Rose / Magenta):** The signature maternal accent. Used for mother & newborn highlights, high-priority care alerts, badge indicators, and empathetic calls-to-action.
- **Soft Blush Tint (`#FDF2F4`):** Soft foundational background for secondary chips, newborn vitals panels, and active row highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`, Royal Warm Gold):** Reflects Riyadh executive excellence, VIP care tiers, and verification seals. Accentuated alongside `#F59E0B` for star ratings and certified nurse seals.
- **Emerald & Mint Accents (`#059669`, `#10B981`, `#ECFDF5`):** Express verified status, completed bookings, normal medical readings, and live attendant availability.
- **Canvas & Neutral Base (`#FBFBF9`, Warm Cream / Ivory):** The soft ambient background replacing harsh clinical stark white. Supported by `#F4F4F0` for card borders and `#1F2937` for crisp data table typography.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dual rhythm:
1. **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Elevates executive dashboards, service titles, analytical summaries, and mother/infant profile headlines. Its graceful curves evoke care, safety, and maternal warmth.
2. **Precision Sans-Serif (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean optical legibility for intensive administrative tasks: nurse dispatch schedules, medical checklist logs, shift timelines, and Saudi billing tables.

### Rules for Application
- Never use Playfair Display on data tables, inputs, button labels, or micro-metadata.
- Keep serif headings balanced with modest tracking to preserve dignified elegance.
- Numerical data within financial rosters and vitals metrics should utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The portal layout adheres to a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid with high horizontal stability to support dense administrative work.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout. Standard left navigation rail fixed at 260px (`#022C22` backdrop). Grid gutters set to `gutter` (1.5rem), section canvas margins set to `margin` (2rem). Content cards span 3, 4, 6, or 12 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar collapses to an icon bar (72px). Gutters scale to `gutter-sm` (1rem); canvas margins compress to 1.5rem.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Off-canvas drawer navigation. Outer margins tighten to `margin-mobile` (1rem). Dense table views switch to stacked card architecture.

### Spacing Rhythm
Component vertical flow strictly observes 8pt spatial increments. Internal card padding leverages `space-lg` (1.5rem) for macro-containers and `space-md` (1rem) for condensed nurse-roster items and data filters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows in favor of ambient medical-luxe depth, using multi-layered, low-contrast shadows tinted with deep emerald and warm umber tones.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FBFBF9` warm cream matte background. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Containers):** Pure ivory white `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#FBFBF9`, bounded by a delicate 1px border (`#EBE8E1`). Ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04), 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers & Hover Cards):** Elevated surface with increased diffusion: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Slide-out Drawers):** Used for booking confirmations and care protocol creation. Shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(2, 44, 34, 0.16)`. Accompanied by an ambient backdrop scrim (`rgba(2, 44, 34, 0.45)`) paired with a `4px` blur.

## Shapes

The portal implements balanced, soft geometry (Level 2 roundedness) to balance medical authority with gentle, welcoming shapes appropriate for maternity and childcare.

### Radii Guidelines
- **Controls & Buttons:** 0.5rem (8px) for buttons, text fields, and standard input boxes.
- **Cards & Data Panels:** 1rem (16px) for client records, booking cards, and service dashboards.
- **Badges, Avatar Rings & Status Pills:** Full pill-shape (`rounded-full` or 9999px) for dispatch status, newborn vitals indicators, and tags.
- **Borders:** Featherweight 1px outlines (`#EBE8E1`) retain clean structure without heavy graphical noise.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Attendant / Confirm Care):** Deep Forest Green (`#064E3B`) fill, crisp white typography, subtle gold focus ring (`#D97706`). Hover state shifts to `#022C22`. Height: 42px; border-radius: 8px.
- **Secondary Action (Maternal / Infant Care Alerts):** Soft Blush Pink background (`#FDF2F4`), Rose text (`#BE185D`), 1px rose-tint border (`#FCE7F3`). Hover state darkens background to `#FCE7F3`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost (Table Controls / Print Log):** Transparent background, Forest Green text, subtle `#EBE8E1` hover border.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Confirmed / Normal Vitals:** Emerald mint tint (`#ECFDF5`), emerald text (`#059669`), subtle emerald ring. Full pill border-radius.
- **Pending Dispatch / Caregiver Assigned:** Amber gold tint (`#FEF3C7`), deep gold text (`#92400E`).
- **Maternal / High Priority Check:** Rose blush tint (`#FDF2F4`), vibrant magenta text (`#BE185D`).

### Data Tables & Roster Lists
- **Header:** Background `#F4F4F0`, labels set in `label-sm` (uppercase, letter-spaced) in forest neutral `#4B5563`.
- **Rows:** Alternating rows remain white; active/selected rows wash with `#FDFBF7` bordered with a 2px left accent line in Forest Green.
- **Cell Content:** Numerical dates and times set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` tabular numbers. Care category chips aligned to right edge.

### Form Inputs & Date/Time Selectors
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#D1CEB8` border. Placeholder text set in muted slate (`#9CA3AF`). Focus state transitions to `#064E3B` border accompanied by an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.12)` halo.
- **Labels:** Set in `title-sm` with optional required indicator in rose (`#BE185D`).

### Care Detail Cards
- Ivory white backgrounds framed with 16px corner radius. Card headers feature small gold emblem accents (`#D97706`) and serif title formatting (`headline-sm`).
- Top-right corner anchors client VIP badge or infant stage marker (e.g., "Newborn - Week 2").
